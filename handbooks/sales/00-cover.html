The words you will see

WordMeaning
FileOne group’s trip: the agent, the dates, the people and every service. Each file has its own number.
AgentThe travel agency that sends us the group and pays us.
Profit %The markup on cost. A cost of 100 at 30% sells for 130.
MarginThe profit as a share of the selling price. The same 30% markup is a 23.1% margin. Both are right; they measure different things.
RQ / OKRequested (waiting for the hotel) / Confirmed (the hotel said yes and gave its number).
Conf. No.The hotel’s own confirmation number. A hotel cannot be marked confirmed without it.
ProformaThe price we send the agent. The accountant turns it into the real invoice.
