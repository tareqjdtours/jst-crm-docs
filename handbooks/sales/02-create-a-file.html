Creating a file

An agent has booked a group. Open a file for it: everything about the trip goes in that file.

WhoSales
WhereIncoming → Reservations
Takes3 to 5 minutes
You needThe agent’s booking: group name, dates, flights and how many people

How it works

One group is one file. The file holds the agent, the dates and the people. Hotels, transport, guides and the rest are added to it afterwards (guides 3 and 4). The profit % you type here fills in the selling price of every service you add.

Example in this guide: Example Group C, the Rossi family and friends, sent by Example Holidays Ltd (their ref EH-2026-118). 4 adults, Italian, 5 to 9 December 2026, flights RJ 102 in and RJ 101 out, 30% profit.

1. Open a new file

1
Open Incoming → Reservations and click New.
2
Type a Group Name everyone will recognise, and the client’s name. Pick the Agent and type the agent’s own reference for the booking.
3
Fill the arrival and departure dates, and the times and flights if you know them. Then type how many adults, children and infants, and the nationality.
The agent has no account yet?Type their name in New Agent instead. Saving the file opens an account for them under Agents. Fill in its details afterwards (guide 1).
4
Scroll down. Pick the Reservation Owner (who looks after this file) and type the Profit %. Click Save.

2. The file is open

5
The file is saved and opens. Its number is next to the agent’s name; give it to the agent with your reply. The tabs along the bottom are where you add the services.

3. Check your work

  • The dates and the number of nights are right.
  • The agent and their reference are on the file.
  • The owner is the person who will follow the booking.
