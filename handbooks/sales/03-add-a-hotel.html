Adding a hotel to a file

Put the group’s stay on the file: which hotel, which nights, which rooms, and what it costs.

WhoSales
WhereThe file → Hotels tab
Takes2 minutes per stay
You needThe hotel’s rate for the stay

How it works

Each stay is one line on the Hotels tab. A group staying in two hotels has two lines. You type the cost (what the hotel charges us), and the selling price to the agent fills in from the file’s profit %.

Example in this guide: Example Hotel Amman, 5 to 9 December, 4 people in 2 double rooms, bed and breakfast, cost 480 JOD for the whole stay. At 30% the selling price fills in as 624 JOD.

1. Add the stay

1
Open the file, go to the Hotels tab and click Add hotel.
2
Pick the hotel and type the check-in and check-out dates. Fill the number of people, the meal plan and the room type.
3
Type how many rooms of each kind: SGL single, DBL double, TRP triple, TWN twin. Leave Status on RQ — REQUESTED while you wait for the hotel.

2. Price and save

4
Type the Cost: what the hotel charges for the whole stay. Selling fills in from the profit %. Click Save.
A special rate?If the hotel gave you a special price, tick Special rate, type the special cost and selling, and say why. The standard rate is kept beside it, so everyone can see what changed and who agreed it.
5
The stay is on the file. Click the row whenever you need to change it.

3. Check your work

  • The nights match the file’s dates.
  • The rooms add up to the number of people.
  • Cost is for the whole stay, not one night.
