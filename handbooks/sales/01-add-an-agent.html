Adding a new agent

A travel agency wants to send us groups. Before you can open a file for them, they need an account.

WhoSales
WhereIncoming → Accounts
Takes2 to 3 minutes
You needThe agency’s name, phone, e-mail, country and the payment terms you agreed

How it works

Every company we work with is an account: agents, hotels, transport companies, restaurants and guides. An agent’s account holds their contact details and their terms. Every file you open for them, and every invoice they get, points to it.

Example in this guide: Example Holidays Ltd, an agency in Rome, with a credit limit of 5,000 JOD and 30 days to pay.

Look firstSearch the Accounts list for the agency’s name before you add it. Two accounts for one agent split their invoices and payments in two.

1. Open a new account

1
Open Incoming → Accounts and click New Account.
2
Type the agency’s company name. Set Main Account to Agents and Group to AG — Agents. Then fill the phone and the e-mail they use for bookings, and the contact person.
“That category belongs to another group”Leave Category empty unless you know the right one. A category belongs to one group, and the form refuses one from another group.

2. Terms and save

3
Fill the country and city. Type the Credit Limit (the most they may owe us at one time) and the Payment Due Days (how many days they have to pay an invoice). Click Save.
4
The new agent is in the Agents tab, with its own number. You can now pick it on a file.

3. Check your work

  • The agent appears under Agents with the right name.
  • The e-mail is the bookings address, not a person who may leave.
  • Credit limit and payment days match what you agreed.
