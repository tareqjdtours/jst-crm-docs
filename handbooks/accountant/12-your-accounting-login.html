Your accounting login

One login for the CRM and one for the Quota Dashboard. Here is how to sign in the first time, and what each one lets you do.

WhoAccountants
WhereThe CRM and the Quota Dashboard sign-in pages
Takes5 minutes
You needThe two temporary passwords from an administrator

1. Sign in the first time

1
An administrator gives you a temporary password for each system. They are different.
2
Sign in to the CRM with the accounting e-mail address and its temporary password. The CRM asks you to choose your own password straight away.
3
Open the Quota Dashboard. Cloudflare first asks for the accounting e-mail address and sends a code to that mailbox. Then sign in with Quota’s temporary password and choose your own.
Forgotten passwordAsk an administrator to issue a new temporary password from the Users page. Never share your password, even with a colleague.

2. In the CRM: the Accounting role

You can

  • See every reservation file and its Finance tab.
  • Post and unpost a file’s service lines.
  • Post invoices, refunds, credit notes, receipts, payments and journals, and convert proformas.
  • Approve or reject drafts in the Inbox.
  • Reverse, unpost or delete a voucher (guide 1).
  • Approve a proforma price adjustment.
  • Send an invoice to JoFotara, once the office switches sending on. It is a real tax document and cannot be taken back.
  • Open every accounting report, and the files Profit and Loss with cost and profit.
  • See files and vouchers in closed years.
  • Change agent and supplier accounts.

You cannot

  • Create or change reservation files, service lines or passengers.
  • See passport numbers and dates of birth. They show masked.
  • Reopen a closed year.
  • Change setup lists, system switches, backups or users.
  • See the Change log.

3. In the Quota Dashboard: read only, with money

You can

  • See every order, with its cost, profit and margins.
  • Browse the catalogue: day tours, hotels, rates and transport.
  • See orders in closed years.

You cannot

  • Build, change or send offers.
  • Send an order to the CRM.
  • Change the catalogue, rates, settings or users.
  • Connect a mailbox or use the Email tab.
Need more?Each of these is a tick an administrator can add or remove for you on the Users page. Ask the finance manager first.
