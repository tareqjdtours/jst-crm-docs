The guides

  1. 1
    Fixing a mistake on a voucherEdit, unpost, reverse or delete a voucher.
  2. 2
    Approving a supplier invoiceOperations record it on the file; you approve it in the Inbox.
  3. 3
    Invoicing an agentConvert Sales’ proforma, or invoice a file from the Inbox.
  4. 4
    Recording money from an agentPost the receipt and match it to the invoice.
  5. 5
    Paying a supplierPay one supplier now, by cheque, transfer, cash or card.
  6. 6
    Paying several suppliers at onceA payment run from Inbox → To pay.
  7. 7
    Booking a bill by handA bill that is not on the file, such as an extra transfer.
  8. 8
    Statement of accountWhat an agent or supplier owes, and sending it to them.
  9. 9
    Checking the trial balanceDebits must equal credits, every month.
  10. 10
    Closing the yearMove the year’s result to Profit & Loss (retained).
  11. 11
    The new year before the old one is closedWork in either year from 1 January; the reports carry the old year in.

The words you will see

WordMeaning
InboxYour to-do list: To approve, To invoice, To pay, Unmatched money.
DraftWaiting for approval. No number yet, in no figure.
PostedIn the books. It counts in every balance and report.
Not postedSaved with a number, but in no balance or report yet.
Open / PaidAn invoice still owed / an invoice settled by a payment.
Dr / CrOn an agent: Dr means they owe us. On a supplier: Cr means we owe them.
