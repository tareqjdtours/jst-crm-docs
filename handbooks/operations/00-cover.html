The words you will see

WordMeaning
To assignA transport or guide line Sales have asked for, with no company or guide yet. That is your queue.
RequestThe Sales half of a line: day, time, route, people, what is needed.
Run downA printed list of the day’s movements, for the desk or a supplier.
RQ / OKRequested (waiting for the supplier) / Confirmed (the supplier said yes and gave its number).
Conf. No.The supplier’s own confirmation number. A line cannot be marked confirmed without it.
autoA reminder the system raised by itself from the arrival date.
