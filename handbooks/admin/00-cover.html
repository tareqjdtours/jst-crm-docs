The words you will see

WordMeaning
PermissionOne thing a person may do, such as “open the reservation list” or “see cost and selling”. There are 67.
Temporary passwordThe first password the CRM makes for someone. It is shown once, and they replace it with their own at their first sign-in.
Reservation ownerThe name on a file of the person it belongs to. Someone who does not see every file sees only the files with their name here.
Active / InactiveWhether a person can sign in. Someone who has left is switched off, not deleted, so their work stays on record.
